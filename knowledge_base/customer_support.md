# NovaTech Customer Support Policy

NovaTech provides customer support to help authorized users resolve technical issues, understand product behavior, and use purchased features effectively. Support channels, hours, response targets, and service levels vary by subscription plan and are listed in the customer’s agreement or support portal.

Customers should search NovaTech’s documentation and service-status page before opening a case. A support request should include the account name, affected users, a clear description, steps to reproduce the issue, relevant timestamps, expected behavior, and screenshots or logs that do not expose unnecessary sensitive data. Customers must not submit passwords, private authentication keys, or regulated data unless specifically instructed through an approved secure channel.

NovaTech assigns priority according to severity and business impact. Critical priority generally means a production service is unavailable for most users or a confirmed security event is actively causing harm. High priority covers major degradation without a reasonable workaround. Standard priority covers limited-impact defects, configuration questions, and general guidance. NovaTech may adjust priority after reviewing the facts.

Initial response targets measure the time until support acknowledges and begins assessing a case; they do not guarantee resolution within that period. Resolution time depends on reproducibility, complexity, third-party dependencies, and customer cooperation. Customers must provide timely information, maintain a knowledgeable contact, and test proposed solutions when reasonable.

Support does not include custom development, management of third-party systems, formal training, data entry, or strategic consulting unless separately purchased. NovaTech may close cases that are resolved, duplicated, outside support scope, or awaiting customer response for an extended period. Closed cases may be reopened or referenced in a new request.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported through NovaTech’s designated security channel rather than ordinary support. Customer-facing incident updates will be provided through appropriate status or account communications.
