# NovaTech Pricing Policy

NovaTech uses transparent, consistent pricing for its software products and services. Current prices, included usage, billing intervals, and applicable limits are presented in the customer’s order form, online checkout page, or signed agreement. Unless otherwise stated, prices exclude taxes, duties, bank charges, and optional professional services.

Subscriptions may be billed monthly or annually in advance. Annual plans may include a discount in exchange for a longer commitment. Usage-based charges are calculated from NovaTech’s system records and added to the next invoice. Customers are responsible for reviewing plan limits and monitoring authorized user activity.

NovaTech may offer free trials, promotional rates, nonprofit pricing, or startup discounts. Such offers may have eligibility requirements, expiration dates, usage limits, and renewal conditions. Promotions cannot be combined unless expressly stated. When a trial ends, paid service begins only according to the acceptance and billing terms shown at signup.

Customers may upgrade at any time. Upgrade charges are generally prorated for the remaining billing period. Downgrades normally take effect at the next renewal and may reduce access to features, storage, or support. NovaTech will provide reasonable notice before a pricing change applies to an existing subscription, except when immediate changes are required by law, tax rules, or third-party fees.

Invoices are due on the date stated in the applicable agreement. Overdue accounts may be subject to reminders, late charges where permitted, service restriction, or suspension. Customers should contact Billing promptly to dispute an invoice; undisputed amounts remain payable.

Fees are nonrefundable except as required by law or stated in the customer agreement. If this policy conflicts with a signed order form or master agreement, the signed agreement controls. NovaTech may correct obvious pricing errors before accepting an order.
