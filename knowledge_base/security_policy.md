# NovaTech Information Security Policy

Every NovaTech employee, contractor, and authorized partner is responsible for protecting company systems, confidential information, customer data, and personal information. Access must be used only for legitimate business purposes and only to the extent required for assigned duties.

Users must create strong, unique passwords and use company-approved password-management tools. Multi-factor authentication is required wherever available. Passwords, authentication codes, access badges, and encryption keys must never be shared. Devices must be locked whenever left unattended, even briefly.

Company work must be performed on approved devices and services. Employees may not move confidential information to personal email, consumer file-sharing sites, unapproved artificial-intelligence tools, or removable storage. Sensitive information must be encrypted when stored or transmitted and shared only with verified recipients who have a business need to know.

Employees must install required security updates promptly and may not disable antivirus software, endpoint monitoring, firewalls, or device-management controls. Public Wi-Fi should be avoided for sensitive work unless an approved virtual private network is active. Lost or stolen devices must be reported immediately.

Suspected phishing, malware, unauthorized access, accidental disclosure, or other security incidents must be reported to the Security team as soon as discovered. Employees should preserve relevant messages and files but must not investigate, delete evidence, or communicate externally about an incident without authorization. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

NovaTech may monitor company systems and accounts as permitted by law to maintain security and investigate misuse. Access is reviewed regularly and removed when no longer needed. Violations may lead to restricted access, disciplinary action, termination, or legal consequences. Employees must complete assigned security training and acknowledge this policy annually.
