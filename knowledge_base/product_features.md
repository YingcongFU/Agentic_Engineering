# NovaTech Product Features Policy

NovaTech develops cloud-based tools that help teams organize work, collaborate securely, automate routine processes, and understand operational performance. Available features depend on the customer’s plan, region, configuration, and contract. The current plan description or order form identifies the features included in a purchase.

Core capabilities may include shared workspaces, project and task management, real-time collaboration, search, file attachments, notifications, dashboards, reporting, workflow automation, and integrations with approved third-party services. Administrative features may include user management, access roles, activity records, security settings, and usage controls.

NovaTech may release features in preview, beta, early-access, or experimental form. Such features are optional, may be incomplete, and can change or be discontinued without the notice normally provided for generally available features. Customers should not rely on preview features for critical operations unless NovaTech agrees otherwise in writing.

Some functionality uses artificial intelligence to summarize, classify, recommend, or generate content. AI-generated output may be inaccurate and must be reviewed by a qualified person before it is used for important business, legal, financial, employment, safety, or compliance decisions. Customers are responsible for ensuring their use of AI features is lawful and appropriate.

Integrations depend on third-party platforms that NovaTech does not control. Their availability, permissions, and behavior may change. Customers must maintain any required third-party accounts and comply with the applicable third-party terms.

NovaTech continuously improves the product and may modify interfaces, workflows, or supporting technology. When practical, NovaTech will provide advance notice of changes that materially reduce a generally available core feature. Documentation describes intended operation but does not create a warranty beyond the customer’s agreement. Feature requests are welcomed but do not create a commitment or delivery date unless included in a signed contract.
