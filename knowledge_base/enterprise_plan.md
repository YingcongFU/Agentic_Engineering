# NovaTech Enterprise Plan Policy

The NovaTech Enterprise Plan is designed for organizations that require advanced security, centralized administration, higher service commitments, and tailored deployment support. Enterprise availability, pricing, and minimum commitments are provided through a written order form and may vary according to user count, usage, data residency, integrations, and support requirements.

Enterprise customers receive organization-level administration, role-based access controls, single sign-on, user provisioning options, audit logs, and configurable data-retention settings where supported. Certain features may require compatible identity providers, customer configuration, or additional technical review. Customers remain responsible for managing authorized users and assigning appropriate permissions.

NovaTech may offer enhanced support, onboarding, solution architecture, migration assistance, and administrator training. The specific services, response targets, support hours, and named contacts are defined in the applicable agreement. A service-level agreement applies only when expressly included in the order form.

Enterprise customers may request security documentation, compliance reports, vendor questionnaires, and data-processing terms. Access to confidential audit materials may require a nondisclosure agreement. Custom security reviews and specialized legal or technical commitments are subject to approval and may involve additional fees.

Unless otherwise agreed, Enterprise subscriptions have an annual term and renew according to the order form. Adding users or capacity during the term may create prorated charges. Reductions normally take effect at renewal and must be requested before the contractual notice deadline.

NovaTech and the customer will each designate an administrative contact. The customer must provide accurate deployment information, timely access to relevant personnel, and reasonable cooperation during implementation. Product functionality may evolve, but NovaTech will not materially reduce purchased core capabilities during the committed term without providing an appropriate remedy under the agreement.

Signed agreements control over this summary whenever terms differ.
