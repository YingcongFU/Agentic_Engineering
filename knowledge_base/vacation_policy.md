# NovaTech Vacation Policy

NovaTech encourages employees to take meaningful time away from work to rest, recharge, and attend to personal responsibilities. Regular full-time employees receive paid vacation according to their employment agreement and local requirements. Part-time employees may receive prorated vacation based on scheduled hours and applicable law.

Employees should submit vacation requests through the company’s time-off system as early as possible. Requests for one or two days should normally be submitted at least five business days in advance. Requests for three or more consecutive days should normally be submitted at least three weeks in advance. Emergencies and unexpected circumstances will be considered individually.

Managers approve requests based on team coverage, business deadlines, fairness, and the order in which requests were received. Employees should not purchase nonrefundable travel until approval is confirmed. During high-demand periods, managers may limit overlapping absences or ask employees to select alternative dates.

Before beginning vacation, employees must communicate current priorities, identify a backup contact, and provide any information needed to maintain essential operations. Employees are not expected to monitor routine messages while on approved vacation. A manager may contact an employee only for a genuinely urgent matter when no reasonable alternative exists.

Vacation balances, carryover rules, and any annual caps are displayed in the time-off system and may vary by location. Employees should use available vacation regularly. Unused vacation is paid out at separation only when required by law or the applicable employment agreement.

Vacation may not be borrowed against future accrual without written Human Resources approval. Misreporting vacation or working elsewhere during leave in a way that creates a conflict of interest may result in corrective action. This policy does not replace legally protected leave or company sick-leave programs.
