# NovaTech Employee Benefits Policy

NovaTech provides a competitive benefits program designed to support employees’ health, financial security, professional growth, and overall well-being. Regular full-time employees are eligible for core benefits beginning on the first day of the month following their start date. Part-time and temporary employees may receive benefits based on local law, scheduled hours, and the terms of their employment agreement.

Eligible employees may enroll in medical, dental, and vision coverage for themselves and qualified dependents. NovaTech pays a significant portion of employee premiums, while dependent coverage and optional plans may require payroll contributions. Employees may also elect tax-advantaged health and dependent-care accounts where available.

NovaTech offers employer-paid basic life insurance, short-term disability coverage, and access to confidential mental-health and employee-assistance resources. Additional voluntary insurance may be purchased during enrollment periods.

To support long-term financial health, eligible employees may participate in the company retirement plan. Employer contributions, vesting schedules, and annual limits are described in the official plan documents. NovaTech may also provide equity awards or performance bonuses depending on role, location, and company results; these are not guaranteed unless stated in writing.

Employees receive a yearly professional-development allowance for approved courses, certifications, conferences, or job-related materials. Additional benefits may include wellness programs, commuter assistance, parental leave, flexible work arrangements, and company-paid holidays.

Employees must make benefit elections within the stated enrollment window. Changes outside that period generally require a qualifying life event. Official plan documents and applicable law control if they conflict with this summary. NovaTech may amend its benefits offerings at any time, with advance notice when reasonably possible.
